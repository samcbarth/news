Football Manager 27 is out on PC, consoles, Netflix and Apple Arcade

Sports Interactive released all four versions of FM27 on November 10, including through Game Pass, Netflix and Apple Arcade subscriptions.

By The Quillmark staff · Nov 11, 2026

When did Football Manager 27 come out?

Football Manager 27 launched on November 10, 2026. Sports Interactive released all digital versions on all supported platforms that day, and SEGA publishes the game.

Which versions are there?

There are four: FM27 for PC and Mac, FM27 Console for Xbox and PlayStation 5, FM27 Touch for Apple devices, and FM27 Mobile. The PC and Mac version is sold on Steam, the Epic Games Store and the Microsoft Store.

Can I play it with a subscription I already have?

Yes, depending on the version. Sports Interactive lists FM27 on Xbox Game Pass and PC Game Pass, the Touch version on Apple Arcade, and the Mobile version through Netflix.

What is new this year?

The studio highlights a reworked interface on PC and Mac, in-game events such as cup draws and transfer deadline day that follow the real football calendar, smarter recruitment and a new Match Preparation feature on console, a revamped iPhone layout for Touch, and a 2D match engine on Mobile.

Was there a way to try it first?

A free Launch Preview ran on PC and Mac before release and ended at 6 p.m. GMT on November 3. Players who already own the game on one subscription service should check that service's library rather than buying it again.
