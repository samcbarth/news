Spotify reaches 306 million paying subscribers and 788 million users

Spotify's third-quarter results show growth in every region, with revenue of 5.0 billion euros and operating income ahead of its own forecast.

By The Quillmark staff · Nov 11, 2026

How many people use Spotify now?

Spotify reported 788 million monthly active users and 306 million Premium subscribers for the third quarter of 2026, in results released October 22. The company said subscriber growth came from every region.

How did the business do?

Revenue was 5.0 billion euros. Gross margin was 33.0% and operating income was 672 million euros, and Spotify said both came in ahead of its expectations.

What else did Spotify add this quarter?

The company said it expanded audiobooks to more than 180 markets by year end.

What does it mean for subscribers?

Spotify's paid base is still growing. Anyone watching a monthly streaming budget can check whether a family or duo plan costs less than separate accounts in the same household.
