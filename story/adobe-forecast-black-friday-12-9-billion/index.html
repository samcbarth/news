Adobe expects Black Friday online sales of $12.9 billion, growing faster than Cyber Monday

Adobe's holiday forecast puts U.S. online spending at a record $275.1 billion for November and December, with the deepest discounts during Cyber Week.

By The Quillmark staff · Nov 11, 2026

How much will people spend online this holiday season?

Adobe forecasts U.S. online sales of $275.1 billion from November 1 to December 31, 2026, up 6.7% from last year. Cyber Week, the five days from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday, is expected to bring in $47.5 billion, up 7.4%.

Which day is the biggest?

Cyber Monday, November 30, is forecast at $15.1 billion, up 6.2%. Black Friday, November 27, is second at $12.9 billion but growing faster, up 9.2%, and Thanksgiving is forecast at $6.9 billion.

When are the best discounts?

Adobe expects discounts of up to 30% off listed prices during Cyber Week. It expects the deepest deals on electronics and computers on Cyber Monday, while the best prices on TVs, toys, apparel, appliances and furniture should land on Black Friday. Discounts are expected to reach up to 14% in early November and up to 21% just before Thanksgiving.

What are shoppers buying?

Electronics ($63.3 billion) and apparel ($51.3 billion) are the largest categories. Grocery is forecast to grow fastest among the major categories, up 10.3%, and Adobe expects many shoppers to use Cyber Week deals to stock up on essentials such as cleaning supplies and pet products.

Should I wait to buy?

For a TV or appliance, Adobe's data points to Black Friday; for a laptop or phone, Cyber Monday. These are forecasts based on past patterns, so tracking a specific item's price over the next few weeks is still the safest way to know you are getting a deal.
