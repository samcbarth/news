Walmart's first Black Friday deals event starts November 12

Walmart is running three holiday sale events this year: early deals Nov. 12 to 15, Black Friday deals Nov. 23 to 29 and Cyber Monday deals Nov. 30 to Dec. 2.

By The Quillmark staff · Nov 11, 2026

When does Walmart's Black Friday sale start?

Walmart's first event, Early Black Friday Deals, runs November 12 to 15, 2026. It starts on Walmart.com at 12:01 a.m. Eastern and in stores at 6 a.m. local time, according to Walmart's announcement.

What are the other two events?

Black Friday Deals run November 23 to 29, starting online at 12:01 a.m. Eastern on November 23 and in stores at 6 a.m. local time on November 27. Cyber Monday deals run online from November 30 to December 2.

Do Walmart+ members get anything extra?

Yes. Walmart+ members get early access to the most popular deal drops at 12:01 a.m. Eastern on November 12 and November 23. Everyone else gets the remaining items 24 hours later, on November 13 and November 24.

How should I shop it?

Make a short list and note today's prices before the events open, so you can tell a real markdown from a normal price. If an item you want is a hot deal drop, an early-access membership only pays off if the savings beat its cost.
