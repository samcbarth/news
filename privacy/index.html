Privacy Policy

Last updated Nov 11, 2026. The Quillmark does not ask you to create an account. We use analytics and, when enabled, Google ads, both of which can set cookies.

What data is collected?

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand which pages are read. These tools collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and referring site. We do not collect names or email addresses unless you send them to us through the contact page.

How are cookies and Google ads used?

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

What about affiliate links?

Headlines on this page link to other samcbarth.com network sites. Some of those sites, such as the streaming, home repair and deals sites, contain affiliate links, including Amazon Associates links, that may earn a commission. Those sites disclose this on every page that has such links.

What about the dark mode setting?

If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
